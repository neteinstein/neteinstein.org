Leadership, high-performing teams and feedback aren't three separate trainings: they're three sides of the same team. That's why they are covered together, in a **full-day workshop**, with the whole team in the room.

The day mixes games, real stories and open conversation. Nobody gets a lecture on what a good team looks like: every game ends with a debrief on what happened, what it means for the team and what changes on Monday morning, so **the team reaches its own conclusions**.

Each day is shaped around the team in it. Below is a taste of each theme and a few of the games and stories it can use, then the wider toolbox a day can draw on. How the games work stays a surprise: half their value is not seeing them coming.
