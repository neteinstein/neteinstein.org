Every game has a card with the group size, the recommended age, how long it takes and whether it is for indoors or outdoors, followed by the materials, the setup, the step-by-step rules, variations and tips, and an illustration of the main rule. Some games were collected from short videos, others from written sources. Each card says where it came from.

**General tip:** always have a timer (your phone's will do), a speaker with music, painter's tape to mark lines on the floor, and spare plastic cups and balloons. With those you can run more than half of the games.
