## Why "neteinstein.org"?

Well, actually "neteinstein" is a username I've used since I was very young (it was actually the lamer NeTeInStEiN version... 😬).

In 1999, [IRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Relay_Chat) was "the thing". I started on DALnet and then moved to [PTnet](http://www.ptnet.org). Finding a free username wasn't easy. I was very fond of Einstein, but it was taken, so... I ended up with this one. Since it was available on (almost) every platform, I kept using it... to this day, I guess.
