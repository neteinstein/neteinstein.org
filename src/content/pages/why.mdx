I've used **neteinstein** since I was very young.

During 1999 I started using the name on IRC, at Dalnet, and then moved to PTnet. Finding a
free username wasn't very easy — I was fond of Einstein, but _einstein_ was taken, so I
ended up with **neteinstein**.

Since it was available on almost every platform, I just kept using it.
