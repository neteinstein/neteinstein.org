I've worked with a lot of different people, from small teams to very large groups (500+), at various organizations, both companies and NGOs. This made me realize that people are my passion. Since then, work has become almost an "excuse" to be with people, get to know them and make work enjoyable for everyone.

Since I do a ton of different things, it's hard to "box me" into a title... so I started calling myself an Improver.

<Bubble>What kind of bullsh\*t title is that? — you might be wondering.</Bubble>

Well, I try to improve everything around me, whether it's team-related or tech-related, small or big. That's always my goal: to help improve and make things better. **Teams are one of my favorite topics that I've focused on for the last 10 years.**
