I could introduce myself and talk about my degree or experience, but [you can see all that below](https://www.linkedin.com/in/neteinstein/)... so let's skip that and get to the point.

What you can't see is that I'm a driven software engineer with a deep interest in software development and a real focus on architecture. I take real pleasure in building happy teams, talking to customers and providing them with the best solution for their needs while delivering good code.

I've seen that software development teams can enjoy the process of building a solution when they use good agile methodologies.

For this to happen, developers, team leaders and product owners/project managers should work closely together towards a better solution. That's why I'm always interested in learning more:

- about people and how teams can work better together
- about agile software development methodologies that improve the development process.

Creating top-notch software isn't just a matter of getting a bunch of people together and telling them to start hitting the keyboard, crazy-nerd-movie style (wink to the Matrix image above).

I value a strong human-first company culture. I believe it can either push a company towards innovation and excellence (if it's good)... OR push it off the cliff, if it's not.

I also value procedures and freedom (yes, agile, procedure and freedom in the same sentence are possible). When things get tricky, each team should be able to choose between keeping their current way of doing things and changing to a better one (but they should have a "current way of doing things").

I really like:

- Being an account manager throughout the process (pre-sales, requirements, development, roadmap and acceptance)
- Leading mobile projects, from architecture to day-to-day
- Coaching teams (development process, feedback, etc.)
