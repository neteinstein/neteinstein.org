<Hobby id="o-que-arde-cura">

Tiago Bahia classified me as a "sadist with a good heart". That is probably because for a long time I used hot sauces to make people have "unique" experiences.

During the COVID lockdown, already foreseeing its heartbreaking consequences, I decided it was time to act.

I challenged some friends: [Tiago Bahia](https://www.facebook.com/tiago.bahia), [Carlos Mota](https://www.cafonsomota.xyz/) and [João Eiró](https://www.facebook.com/pianistaJoaoEiro)... and for some unexplainable reason they accepted the challenge. Not long after, Tiago created the name, [Mariana Leal Rato](https://www.behance.net/marianalealrato) also joined with the brand logo and image: game on!

**"[O Que Arde Cura](http://www.oqueardecura.pt)" was born as our modest contribution to helping those who help others, while trying to make people laugh.**

**How?**

Every month, we interview two guests from a different charity. While they introduce us to their association, we all eat hotter and hotter sauces.

**This is all live to YouTube, Facebook, Twitter... and while we're at it, we ask for donations for the charity. The more donations they get, the hotter the sauce gets!**

**How to define us?**

_We're kind of a Live Aid meets Hot Ones, if Live Aid were to be done by amateurs and Hot Ones had "Frango da Guia" and both of them happened at the same time in someone's living room._

It might last a few months or a lot of them... but it has already been worth it.

Until we decide it's time to put down the fire _we'll definitely "do harm" to a lot of people that do good!_

</Hobby>

<Hobby id="bullshit-buster">

I fight very actively against fake information online.

Why? Simple:

<PullQuote attribution="― Philip Pullman">
  “We should delicately and subtly undermine the idea that truth and facts are possible in the first
  place. Once the people have become doubtful about the truth of anything, all kinds of things will
  be open to us.”
</PullQuote>

I refuse to accept that bullshit can spread untouched just because it takes more effort to fight it. You can quote me on that.

That's why I created a [little section on this site](/hobbies/myths) (still little, but it will grow with time) with myths and fake things that annoy me.

</Hobby>

<Hobby id="gdg-porto">

In June 2015 I co-founded GDG Porto with my good friend [João Raimundo](https://twitter.com/J0t4R41mund0) ✊. Soon joined by [André Silva](https://twitter.com/andre_mr_silva), the "original gang" went on to bring Google Developer Group conferences to Porto.

Our mission?

To be a community that helps Porto developers spread knowledge between people in different companies, schools and universities. Now younger blood has joined the crew (👋 [Artur](https://twitter.com/arturc_dev) and [Nelson](https://twitter.com/SachseInTheCity)) and are actually the new captains (I'm getting old!).

I'm now at their disposal, sir, yes sir!

</Hobby>

<Hobby id="campinacios">

For 10+ years, I was part of Campinácios, a Portuguese summer camp movement born from the three Portuguese Jesuit schools.

During those 10 years, I learned that I loved camping and teaching kids and enjoyed being a summer camp monitor.

I also got deeply involved in the national organization, coordinating both the national and Coimbra leadership groups for some years.

It was such a huge part of my life that, although I'm no longer actively involved, it deserves a place here.

If you're curious about the history of this, check out the [Wikinácios](https://campinacios.pedrovicente.pt).

</Hobby>
